# Privacy Policy — Ratings Badge for Netflix
_Last updated: 8 November 2026_

Ratings Badge for Netflix ("the extension") is an unofficial tool, not affiliated with Netflix or IMDb.

**What it reads:** only the titles of movies and shows visible on netflix.com pages you open, so it can look up their ratings.

**What it sends:** those titles (and a release year when shown) to the IMDb suggestion service (v3.sg.media-imdb.com), Cinemeta (v3-cinemeta.strem.io) and, only if you add your own key, OMDb (omdbapi.com). No account details, viewing history, cookies, or personal identifiers are sent.

**What it stores:** ratings are cached in your browser (chrome.storage.local) to reduce requests. If you choose to enter an OMDb API key, it is stored in Chrome sync storage. Nothing is stored on any server operated by the developer.

**What it does not do:** no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no selling or sharing of data, no remote code.

You can remove all stored data by uninstalling the extension.
